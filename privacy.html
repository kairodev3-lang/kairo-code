Privacy Policy

KAIRO CODE by KAIRO DEV · Effective October 7, 2026

Summary

KAIRO CODE does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location. We do not sell your data.

Data stored on your device

Your projects, files and settings (such as the large-text option) are saved in your browser or app storage on your own device. We do not receive or upload them. Clearing the app data or browser storage deletes them, so use Export to keep a copy.

Data handled when you use the app

  • The app loads from our website, kairo-code.vercel.app, which is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, it may process standard technical data such as your IP address and request details to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  • The Android app is built with a third-party packaging tool that may contact its own server to check whether an app update is available.
  • If you tap Contact Support, you leave the app and open Telegram, which has its own privacy policy.

Your code

Code you write is run in a sandboxed preview on your device. If your own code makes network requests, that is your responsibility, not ours.

Permissions

KAIRO CODE uses internet access to load the app. We do not use your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Children

KAIRO CODE is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do.

Contact
